# Surface brief: MagicArena homepage v2 (pitch)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: parent on a phone deciding an outing or birthday. Action: "Reservá tu visita" (WhatsApp). Proof: real photos of the sand area, Helado Café and real products; no testimonials exist. Constraints: logo intact (PNG only), new vivid accents allowed, user references (ABC Tots, ZemEz FunCare, BrightPath) pin a playful, color-blocked, cut-out-photo kids aesthetic.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a rainbow you walk under. The logo's arcs grow into the architecture: arched photo windows, arc-topped section edges, thick color bands. Refuses the category default of a cream page with a headline, two buttons and three equal icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Deep teal field (#1E5A68) owning the hero; bands of coral (#FF6F59), sunflower (#FFC23C), sky (#9ED4E4) and sand (#F4DCBC); logo leaves and twin rings as floating stickers; Gluten (gooey, sand-like) for display, Nunito for text; arch windows, pill buttons in coral with dark ink.

STORY: Visitor sees the real child in real sand under a rainbow, reads one promise, taps Reservá tu visita; scrolls through what they will find, who runs it, parties, café, shop, community, hours.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Teal field. Left (top on mobile): headline in 2–3 lines, short lede, coral CTA, open-now line. Right (below on mobile): arched photo of the sand child inside 4 concentric color bands, Helado Café round inset, leaves sprouting from the arch top, rotating seal. The innermost band continues as a line ending at the CTA.

FORM: User-pinned references fused with the logo's arc system; no concept roll. seed: none (direction pinned by user references)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
