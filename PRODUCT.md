# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (delegated: chosen by Claude for a pitch prototype; GSAP 3.13.0 + Lenis 1.3.11 self-hosted in `vendor/`, per the Ruva Web Pro workflow the user invoked).

## Users

Parents and caregivers in eastern San José (Curridabat, Pinares, Tres Ríos area) with young children, deciding on a phone where to spend an afternoon or weekend, or where to hold a small birthday. Secondary: schools and occupational therapists looking for a sensory space for groups. (Inferred from the current site's content and the school list; not interviewed.)

The immediate audience of this project is the MagicArena owners: this is a redesign pitch prepared by an outside designer, to be presented as before/after.

## Product Purpose

MagicArena is an indoor playground inside Momentum Pinares (Curridabat, Costa Rica) built around free, unstructured play: a sensory sand area, pretend-play corners (the "Helado Café" play kitchen), weekly rotating activities, plus a café for adults, birthday/play-date hosting, and a small online shop (chalk, magnetic mazes, LCD boards, family caps and shirts) shipped via Correos de Costa Rica. Success for the site: a visiting family understands what the place is, trusts it is real, and reserves a visit over WhatsApp.

## Positioning

Founded by parents ("Somos padres. Creamos el espacio que también queríamos encontrar."). Stimulate without overstimulating: free play at the child's pace, calm and rest when needed, inclusive, with a social-responsibility program that shares the space with children from foundations.

## Operating Context

All conversions happen on WhatsApp (+506 8676 1520) with prefilled messages; email hola@magicarena.cr. Food is ordered in person from staff. Shop orders are confirmed by WhatsApp and paid by payment link, SINPE Móvil or bank transfer.

## Evidence on Hand

- The current site's family and party photos appear not to show the real space; do not reuse them.
- No testimonials, ratings, visitor counts or press. Do not fabricate.

## Product Principles

1. Truth first: only real photos of the real space; unknown facts appear as visible placeholders.
2. The visit is the main action; shop and café support it.
3. Joyful energy without overstimulation, mirroring the space's own promise.
4. Phone first: most visitors arrive from Instagram/WhatsApp on mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast; full content and function with reduced motion or without JavaScript; the place promotes inclusion of children with special needs, so the site should be usable with assistive technology.
